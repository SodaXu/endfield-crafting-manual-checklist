## Design reference

The official site informed the exact yellow `#fffa00`, black / light-gray section contrast, wide uppercase display type, thin technical rules, bracket markers, diagonal hatch accents, rectangular controls, and left navigation. This handbook uses local system fonts and its own unofficial text identity, not the official logo or proprietary fonts.
